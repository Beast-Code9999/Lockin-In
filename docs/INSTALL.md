# Installing Lockin' In (Android)

Lockin' In isn't on the Play Store, so you install it from a file. It takes about two minutes.

## Install
1. Open the **LockinIn-x.y.z.apk** file that was sent to you (from WhatsApp, Drive or email).
2. Android will ask whether to allow installs from that app (e.g. Chrome, Files or WhatsApp). Tap **Settings**, switch on **Allow from this source**, then go back.
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.
   - If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap **More details → Install anyway**. It warns about anything that isn't from the Play Store.

## Join the crew
1. Tap **Let's lock in → Sign in with Google**. Use the Gmail address you gave the crew owner; only listed accounts can sign in for now.
2. Pick your name and character, and check your timezone.
3. On **Your crew**, type the 6-letter invite code you were sent and tap **Join crew**.
4. Read **The Pact**, tick the box and sign.

You're in. Clean check-ins open at **5pm** your time, and you can still check in for yesterday until midnight.

## Updating
When you get a newer APK, open it and tap **Update**. Your account and check-ins are kept.
